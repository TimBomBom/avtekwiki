
Below is a list of common words, phrases, nicknames, etc. for avtek related things.

# General Terms

- Strike - takedown/cleanup after an event
- U-gnd ("U ground") - Three-pronged power cable you plug into an outlet in the wall. It is called this because the ground pin is U shaped. Often an extension cord.
	- U-gnd quad - A four-outlet extension cable with U-gnd connectors
- IEC - The trapezoidal power connector used in some speakers and computers
- XLR - The connector for microphones and microphone cables. Features three pins in a barrel connector
- DMX - The connector for lights. Can either be three-pinned like XLR (in fact XLR cables can work in these systems) or five-pinned in a barrel connector.
